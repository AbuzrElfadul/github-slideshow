# Style lock — Editorial Motion Graphics

Non-photoreal throughout. No lettering rendered inside clips (titles and captions are added in
post); no faces with speaking mouths — characters emote and gesture only, they do NOT talk.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | deep ink navy `#0E1420` | every background |
| Structure | bone white `#EDE6D8` | body forms, cell walls, grid lines |
| **Accent — the repair crew** | warm amber `#F2A93B` | the through-line motes, always |
| Damage | dull coral `#D4614E` | breaks, senescent cells, inflammation |
| Recessive | slate `#3B4757` | depth, shadow, inactive matter |

Amber only ever means *repair*. Coral only ever means *damage*. The whole video is legible from
the ratio of those two colours on screen, and the arc is amber losing ground to coral.

## Style key prompt (`seedream_v5_pro`, `resolution:"1k"`, 16:9)

> Editorial motion-graphics illustration, flat vector with subtle paper grain. Deep ink navy
> background, bone white line forms, warm amber accent, dull coral for damage, slate for depth.
> Geometric and diagrammatic rather than organic. Clean negative space, confident thick strokes,
> limited five-colour palette, no text, no lettering, no photorealism, no gradients beyond a
> single soft vignette. Scientific-poster sensibility, mid-century editorial restraint.
